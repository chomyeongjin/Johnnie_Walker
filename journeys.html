Every Hour, For One Glass.

The Walkers Experiences

MORNING

The day starts slowly: breakfast at the long table, then time in the lobby before you set out.

AM 6:30

Before the Trail

Start the day at one long table with the other guests.
Porridge, a Scottish breakfast and kippers are served from the centre of the table from 6:30.
A morning briefing covers the weather and the route ahead.

AM 7:30

A SLOW START

After breakfast, take your time in the lobby before the trail.
Sit by the low round sofa, check the route with our staff,
and pick up your trail pack when you are ready to leave.

Hiking Programme

Three trails, one for each landscape.
Each stay begins with a trail.
Choose the cliffs, the forest or the glen, and the room, the label and the day are set around it.

  • Trail · Red

    The CliffsEasy · 9.5 km · 3–3.5 h

    AM 9:00 · West to Ardnamurchan

    A two-hour drive west, crossing Loch Linnhe on the Corran Ferry, to the small village of Portuairk.

    AM 11:00 · Ardnamurchan Point

    A coastal loop over moorland, down to the white sands of Bay MacNeil and on to the lighthouse at the westernmost point of mainland Britain. Open your Summit Wee Dram here.

  • Trail · Green

    The ForestsChallenging · 4 km · 2–3 h

    AM 10:00 · South to Strontian

    A one-hour drive across the Corran Ferry to the edge of Scotland’s Atlantic oakwoods.

    AM 11:00 · Ariundle Oakwoods

    Through moss-covered oaks, along the Strontian River and up to the old lead mines, with views back down the glen.

  • Trail · Yellow

    The GlensModerate · 10.5 km · 3–4 h

    AM 10:30 · Into Glen Coe

    A thirty-minute drive south to the Three Sisters car park.

    AM 11:00 · Lost Valley

    A short, steep climb across the River Coe and up a rocky gorge, into the hidden valley where the MacDonalds once hid their cattle.

The Trail Pack

Your pack is waiting at the door: a sandwich, oatcakes, shortbread and a flask of hot tea.
Inside is a Summit Wee Dram – a miniature bottle of your trail’s label, to open at the top.

Afternoon ACTIVITIES

The afternoon is for recovery. Back from the trail, you leave your gear behind, swim by the loch
and rest your legs, so that by dinner the body is ready for the evening’s glass.

PM 4:00

WHERE THE TRAIL ENDS

Return to the Hiking Room and leave your boots,
jacket and pack in the locker marked with your room. Everything is dried, cleaned and ready by morning.

PM 4:30

REST FOR THE LEGS

In the wellness room, a foot massage eases the muscles that carried you,
and whisky aroma therapy prepares your senses for the evening’s glass.

PM 5:30

THE LOCH POOL

A pool at the water’s edge, looking out over the loch to the mountains.
Swim slowly or sit on the deck as the afternoon light changes.

Evening

The evening brings everyone back to the same table. Dinner moves through the three landscapes you walked,
and the day ends with a glass of your own — blended in private or made for you at the kitchen bar.

Dining Programme

PM 7:30

One Table, Three Landscapes

Dinner is one menu for everyone,
moving from the coast to the forest to the glen: Cullen Skink, venison stew, then haggis, neeps and tatties.
The dishes are shared.

  • I. Coast — Cullen Skink

    A soup of smoked haddock, potato and onion,
    from the fishing village of Cullen.

  • II. Forest — Venison Stew

    Highland venison slow-cooked with root vegetables
    and wild mushrooms.

  • III. Glen — Haggis, Neeps & Tatties

    Scotland’s national dish, served with
    mashed swede and potato.

  • Dessert — Cranachan

    Toasted oats, Scottish raspberries, heather honey
    and whipped cream, finished with a dash of whisky.

Blending Programme

Guests eat together at one long table, as travelers once did at an inn.
Breakfast is served before you set out, and dinner when everyone is back from the trail.
The menu is built from Highland produce, and each guest’s whisky is chosen by the trail they walked that day.

PM 9:00

The Blending Room — Private

A private session with our blender.
Taste the malts behind your trail’s label, then blend your own dram.
Your bottle is labelled with your trail, your date and your name, and sent to your home after your stay.

PM 9:00

The Blending Bar — Open to All Guests

Stay with the others at the kitchen counter.
Our bartender makes a cocktail for each guest, built around the trail they walked that day.
