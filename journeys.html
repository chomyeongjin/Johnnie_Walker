Every Hour, For One Glass.

The Walkers Experiences

Morning Programme

The day begins slowly, the way a walk should.
A first look at the loch, a warm breakfast at the long table,
and a quiet moment to choose the trail you will walk today.

AM 6:30

The Loch Walk — Sunrise

A short walk along the water as the mist lifts.
A guide points out the route of each trail across the glen.

AM 7:30

The Long Table — Breakfast

Guests share one long table, as travellers once did at an inn.
Porridge, smoked fish and bread from Highland produce, before you set out.

Hiking Programme

Three trails leave from The Walker’s, each with its own landscape and its own whisky.
Walk at your own pace. Every trail ends back at the inn.

AM 9:00

Three Trails — Choose Your Path

The coast, the glen or the ridge.
Each trail is matched to a whisky you will taste at the end of the day.

PM 12:30

The Bothy — Trail Lunch

A stone shelter halfway along each trail.
A packed lunch and a hot drink before the walk back.

Afternoon Programme

Back from the trail, the afternoon is for rest.
Warm up, slow down, and learn how the landscape you walked becomes whisky.

PM 3:00

The Peat Room — Rest

A sauna, a bath and a fire built from local peat.
Time to recover before the evening begins.

PM 4:30

The Cask House — Tasting

A walk through the casks with our cellar master.
Taste how water, peat and wood shape each malt.

Evening Programme

Everyone comes back to the long table.
Dinner is built from Highland produce, and each course is paired to the trails walked that day.

PM 7:00

The Long Table — Dinner

Venison, langoustines and root vegetables from the glen.
Guests share the stories of their trails across the table.

PM 8:30

The Fireside — Stories

A guide tells the history of the land you crossed today,
and the people who first made whisky here.

Blending Programme

Guests come together at one long table, as travellers once did at an inn.
Breakfast is served before you set out, and dinner when everyone is back from the trail.
The menu is built from Highland produce, and each guest’s whisky is chosen by the trail they walked that day.

PM 9:30

The Blending Room — Private

A private session with our blender.
Taste the malts behind your trail’s label, then blend your own dram.
Your bottle is labelled with your trail, your date and your name, and sent to your home after your stay.

PM 9:30

The Blending Bar — Open to All Guests

Stay with the others at the bar counter.
Our bartender makes a cocktail for each guest, built around the trail they walked that day.
