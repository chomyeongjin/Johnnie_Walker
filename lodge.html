1F

Lobby

The lodge opens onto one long room of stone and charred timber.
Guests arrive, leave their boots by the fire,
and take their first dram before they see their room.

1F

Hiking Room

Where every trail begins and ends.
Maps, boots and gear for all three trails,
and a warm drying room for the walk back.

B1

Wellness

Built into the rock below the lodge.
A peat-fired sauna, a cold plunge fed by the loch,
and quiet rooms to recover after the trail.

1F

Dining

One long table under a timber roof.
The kitchen cooks Highland produce over open fire,
and every plate is paired with the whisky of the trail walked that day.

2F

Rooms

A room for each trail.
Each trail has its own room, and each room takes its colour and materials from that landscape.
Dark walls keep the rooms quiet, so the view and the colour of the trail stand out.
